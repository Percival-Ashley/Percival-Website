About

Percival Ashley Ambundo is a Kenyan digital operations specialist, executive virtual assistant, and marketing professional with experience supporting global organizations and leadership teams.

She currently works with Magic Inc., where she provides high-level administrative and operational support, ensuring efficiency across executive workflows, communication, and client management.

With a background in marketing leadership, Ambundo has managed campaigns and client relationships for organizations across multiple industries, consistently delivering high-performance results and customer satisfaction.

She holds a Bachelor of Arts degree from University of Nairobi, where she studied Political Science, Conflict and Peace Studies, and Sociology.

Beyond her professional work, she is actively engaged in digital conversations around technology, AI, and Kenyan socio-political issues, hosting and contributing to discussions that explore the intersection of digital transformation and society.
